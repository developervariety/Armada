# Verification Checklist

> Template. Copy to `11-verification-checklist.md` in this folder and fill it in for your
> deployment. The filled copy is operator-local and is not committed.

Cover at least:

- The landed commit is an ancestor of the target branch.
- The required Checks passed at the reviewed commit.
- The deployment runs the landed commit, when the work must be live.
- Every deferred item has its own record.
