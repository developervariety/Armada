During long multi-agent work the owner repeatedly asks for progress ("How are things coming along?", "What's left?", "Are you almost done?", "Is it done?"). Give a short, direct answer first (yes/no/what remains), then a compact list or table of in-progress items with honest estimates, and what will happen next. Do not let long silent stretches happen: say in a few words what you are doing. Do not claim completion until verified (tests run, CI green when relevant).
