On 2026-10-05 the owner said: "I feel like you've spiraled out of control. This should be done by now... almost eight hours ... over the past 10 background agents", and acknowledged that his own follow-up requests contributed. How to apply: when many workstreams are open, stop launching new ones, list what is in flight with honest estimates (say they are estimates), land and verify what exists, and put remaining work into a tracked follow-up list for the owner to schedule. Prefer fewer, well-scoped agents with clear file boundaries. When asked "what's left", answer with a short concrete list and time estimate, not a narrative.
